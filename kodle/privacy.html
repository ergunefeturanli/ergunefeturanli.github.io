Kodle Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayane Labs handles information in the Kodle mobile game.

Kodle is a local-first number puzzle game: you guess a hidden number in six tries. The app does not require an account, a login, an email address, or a user profile. Your progress lives on your device. The parts of the app that talk to our own server are the optional leaderboard, the delivery of completed in-app purchases, and occasional coin gifts, all described below; you can play the whole game without the leaderboard or any purchase.

Information We Store on Your Device

Kodle stores gameplay information locally on your device so the app can remember your unfinished board, statistics, score, daily streak, coins, helper charges, and settings such as sound, vibration, language, high-contrast colors, and reminders.

The app also generates a random identifier for your installation and stores it on the device. It is not your device's advertising identifier, and it is not linked to your name, email address, or any account.

This local game data stays on your device, apart from the leaderboard, purchase and gift information described below. You can remove the local copy by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Leaderboard

Kodle has an optional weekly and all-time leaderboard. When it is active, the app sends the following to an Ayane Labs server at api.ayanelabs.com: your scores, the display name you type in yourself, whether you asked to be hidden, and the anonymous installation identifier that the app generates at random.

That identifier is not your name, your email address, your phone number, or your device's advertising identifier. It exists so your own score can be recognised as yours on a later visit. The display name is whatever you type; you are free to use a nickname, and nothing checks it against a real identity.

You can switch on "Hide me from the leaderboard" in the app at any time, which removes your entry from the public list. If you would like your leaderboard record deleted from the server entirely, email us and we will remove it.

The server is operated by Ayane Labs and hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.

The same server may offer you a coin gift from us, inside the replies the app already receives. A gift is either for every player or for one installation — for example when you write to support about a problem (see Support Emails). A gift for one installation is stored with that anonymous identifier and deleted about a month after it ends. The app remembers which gifts it has collected; the server does not.

Reminders and Notifications

If you allow notifications, Kodle schedules reminders on your device: at most one ordinary reminder a day, a reminder on a day your daily streak would otherwise end, and a few reminders if you stop playing (on the 10th, 14th, 21st and 30th day), after which there are none. All of this is scheduled on the device itself; nothing about your reminders is sent to our server.

Earlier test versions of the app registered a push notification token with our server. Since October 2026 the server no longer stores or uses such tokens, and the ones it held have been deleted.

You can turn reminders off at any time in the app settings or in your device's system settings.

Update Check

The app occasionally downloads a small configuration file from GitHub Pages (ergunefeturanli.github.io) to learn whether a newer version is available or required, so the daily puzzle stays in step across devices. The request carries no identifier of yours; like any web request, it lets GitHub see your IP address.

Advertising

Kodle uses Google AdMob to show ads. Ads may include a banner, full-screen ads between rounds, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for coins or helpers.

Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app activity related to ad delivery, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data. This processing is handled by Google according to Google's policies.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Kodle shows a consent message before personalized advertising is used.

You can choose whether to allow personalized ads where this choice is available. If you do not allow personalized ads, ads may still be shown, but they may be less relevant.

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, Kodle may ask for permission to use Apple's advertising identifier through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, Kodle will not access the IDFA for tracking.

In-App Purchases

Kodle offers optional in-app purchases of coin packs. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details, billing address, or store account.

We use Adapty to check which purchases an installation is entitled to. Adapty receives the anonymous installation identifier, the purchase receipt issued by the store, and basic device and country information. It does not receive your name or payment details.

On Android, a payment that the store completes later (for example a pending or cash payment) is delivered through our server. Google notifies us of each completed order through Adapty, and our server stores the order number, the product, the purchase time and the billing country. When your app reports that it is waiting for a payment, the server matches the order to it and records the anonymous installation identifier with the order, so the coins are delivered exactly once. These order records are deleted automatically after 60 days.

Analytics

Kodle uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used in aggregate, for example how many players start and finish a game, which difficulty is played, which screens are opened, and whether ads and purchases succeed. Firebase may process a Firebase-generated app instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from IP address, and in-app event data.

We use this information to improve the game. We do not use it to identify you personally. Test builds, emulators and simulators do not send analytics. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email from the app, the message includes optional technical details: app version, platform, device model, and your player code — the anonymous installation identifier — so we can find your leaderboard record or send you a coin gift if something went wrong. You can delete any of these lines before sending. We use support emails only to respond to feedback, support requests, or bug reports.

Children

Kodle is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

Kodle may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and ad consent support.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for aggregate usage analytics.
  • Adapty for verifying in-app purchases and entitlements.
  • An Ayane Labs server (api.ayanelabs.com, Frankfurt) for the optional leaderboard, the delivery of completed in-app purchases, and coin gifts.
  • GitHub Pages for these legal pages and the update configuration file.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play services for app distribution, if you install the app through those stores.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

This section explains how to have data held by Kodle (Ayane Labs) deleted.

On your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes every local copy: your board, statistics, score, streak, coins and settings. Kodle keeps no account, so there is nothing to close.

On our server. Three things can be there, each tied only to the anonymous installation identifier: your leaderboard record (your chosen display name and your scores); on Android, records of completed purchase orders matched to your installation (order number, product, purchase time, billing country — deleted automatically after 60 days); and any coin gift addressed to your installation (deleted about a month after it ends).

How to ask us to delete them:

  1. Email ayanelabs@gmail.com with the subject Kodle data deletion.
  2. Tell us the display name you used, or send the email from the app (Settings → Email Feedback), which includes your player code, so we can find the records.
  3. We delete them within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept. The leaderboard record, the order records and any gifts are deleted outright. Nothing else about you is stored on our server — there is no account, email address or payment card to keep. Ordinary server access logs may keep an IP address for up to 30 days for security, after which they are discarded.

You can also remove your entry from the public list yourself at any time, without writing to us, by switching on "Hide me from the leaderboard".
