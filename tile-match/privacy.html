Ivora Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayane Labs handles information in the Ivora mobile game.

Ivora is an offline tile-matching puzzle game: tap free tiles to move them to the tray and clear three of a kind. The app does not require an account, a login, an email address, or a user profile. It shows ads (see Advertising) and offers optional in-app purchases (see In-App Purchases). It does not use analytics services. Your progress lives on your device. While your device is offline the game shows no ads. The game contacts a server of ours only in one case: on Android, while a payment is still waiting to be completed by the store.

Information We Store on Your Device

Ivora stores gameplay information locally on your device so the app can remember your level, unfinished board, coins, boosters, collected tiles and albums, chests, badges, statistics, daily streak, and settings such as sound, music, vibration, reminders and language. This data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Ivora shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the game screen, optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for a booster, to continue a level, or to double a chest reward) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and basic device information to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. This processing is handled by Google according to Google's policies. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Ivora shows Google's consent message when the game opens, before personalized advertising is used. If you do not allow personalized ads, ads may still be shown, but they may be less relevant. In those regions you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → "Privacy choices".

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, Ivora asks for permission to use Apple's advertising identifier through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, Ivora will not access the identifier for tracking, and the game works the same. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's settings.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are created and kept on the device; no notification service of ours is involved, and the reminder can be turned off in Settings.

Analytics

Ivora does not use analytics or crash-reporting services. If this changes, this policy will be updated before such a version is released.

In-App Purchases

Ivora offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs, a one-time Starter Pack, and Remove Ads. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details, billing address, or store account.

We use Adapty to check which purchases an installation is entitled to, for example to restore Remove Ads after you reinstall the game. Adapty receives an anonymous installation identifier, the purchase receipt issued by the store, and basic device and country information. It does not receive your name or payment details. Learn more at adapty.io/privacy.

On Android, a payment that the store completes later (for example a pending or cash payment) is delivered through our server. Google notifies us of each completed order through Adapty, and our server stores the order number, the product, the purchase time and the billing country. Only while your game is waiting for such a payment does it ask our server about it, sending the product, the time of the purchase attempt and a random installation identifier created for this purpose; the server matches the order and records that identifier with it so the coins are delivered exactly once. These order records are deleted automatically after 60 days. Players without a waiting payment never contact our server.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email from the app, the message includes optional technical details: the app name and version, your platform and operating system version, and the game language, so we can help with a problem. You can delete these lines before sending. We use support emails only to respond to feedback, support requests, or bug reports.

Children

Ivora is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob for ads, including Google's consent message where consent is required.
  • Adapty for checking in-app purchases (see In-App Purchases).
  • GitHub Pages for this page.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution. If you choose to rate the game, the rating window is shown by Apple or Google.

Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the processing by the services listed above, the game does not send your gameplay data anywhere.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes every copy of your game data. Ivora keeps no account. The only records on our side are Android order records described under In-App Purchases, which hold no name or contact details and are deleted automatically after 60 days. Purchase records are also kept by Apple or Google and by Adapty. Ad data is held by Google; you can manage it through your ad privacy choices and your device settings. For an email you sent us, write to ayanelabs@gmail.com with the subject "Ivora data deletion"; we delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.
