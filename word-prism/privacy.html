Word Prism Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayane Labs handles information in the Word Prism mobile game (a codeword / cryptogram puzzle).

Word Prism is a local-first puzzle game. It does not require an account, a login, an email address, or a user profile. Your progress lives on your device. The parts of the app that talk to our own server are the leaderboard (with its weekly prize) and, on Android, the delivery of completed in-app purchases, both described below.

Information We Store on Your Device

Word Prism stores gameplay information locally on your device so the game can remember your level progress, unfinished boards, coins, unlocked backgrounds, badges, daily puzzle streak, score, language choice, purchases you own, and settings such as sound, music, vibration and reminders.

The app also generates a random identifier for your installation and keeps it in the device's secure storage. It is not your device's advertising identifier, and it is not linked to your name, email address, or any account.

This local game data stays on your device, apart from the leaderboard and purchase information described below. You can remove the local copy by resetting progress in the game, clearing the app data, or uninstalling the app.

Leaderboard

Word Prism has weekly and all-time leaderboards. The app sends your score, the number of puzzles you have finished, your game language and a nickname to an Ayane Labs server at api.ayanelabs.com. You are identified only by the random installation identifier described above — not by your name, email address, phone number or advertising identifier. Your nickname is "Player" plus four digits unless you change it; other players can see your nickname, rank and score, nothing else.

When a week closes, the app asks the same server for your final rank in that week so that a weekly prize in coins can be given inside the game.

We do not sell or share this data, and it is not used for advertising. Weekly scores are deleted after 8 weeks; server backups are kept for up to 14 days. You can delete your leaderboard data at any time in Settings → "Delete my leaderboard data", or by emailing us. The server is operated by Ayane Labs and hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.

Reminders and Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Word Prism schedules them locally on your device. Nothing about your reminders is sent to a server and no push token is registered. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's settings or in your device's system settings.

Update Check

The app occasionally downloads a small configuration file from GitHub Pages (ergunefeturanli.github.io) to learn whether a newer version is available or required. The request carries no identifier of yours; like any web request, it lets GitHub see your IP address.

Advertising

Word Prism uses Google AdMob to show ads: rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for coins, and, from level 16 on, occasional full-screen ads between levels. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the full-screen ads between levels; rewarded ads stay available if you want them.

Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app activity related to ad delivery, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data. This processing is handled by Google according to Google's policies. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Word Prism shows a consent message before personalized advertising is used. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads where this choice is available. If you do not allow personalized ads, ads may still be shown, but they may be less relevant.

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, Word Prism asks for permission to use Apple's advertising identifier through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, Word Prism will not access the IDFA for tracking, and the game works the same.

In-App Purchases

Word Prism offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs, a one-time starter pack, and "Remove Ads". Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details, billing address, or store account.

We use Adapty to check which purchases an installation is entitled to. Adapty receives the anonymous installation identifier, the purchase receipt issued by the store, and basic device and country information. It does not receive your name or payment details.

On Android, a payment that the store completes later (for example a pending or cash payment) is delivered through our server. Google notifies us of each completed order through Adapty, and our server stores the order number, the product, the purchase time and the billing country. When your app reports that it is waiting for a payment, the server matches the order to it and records the anonymous installation identifier with the order, so the coins are delivered exactly once. These order records are deleted automatically after 60 days.

Analytics

Word Prism uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used in aggregate, for example whether ads and purchases succeed and which parts of the game are played. Firebase may process a Firebase-generated app instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from IP address, and in-app event data. We use this information to improve the game; we do not use it to identify you personally. Test builds, emulators and simulators do not send analytics. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email from inside the game, your mail app opens with a message you write yourself. To help us answer, the draft includes the game version, your device name, your operating system version and the game language. You can delete any of it before sending. We use support emails only to answer you.

Children

Word Prism is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

Word Prism uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and ad consent support.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for aggregate usage analytics.
  • Adapty for verifying in-app purchases and entitlements.
  • An Ayane Labs server (api.ayanelabs.com, Frankfurt) for the leaderboard, the weekly prize, and the delivery of completed in-app purchases on Android.
  • GitHub Pages for these legal pages and the update configuration file.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution and payments.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

This section explains how to have data held by Word Prism (Ayane Labs) deleted.

On your device. Resetting progress in the game, uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage removes the local copies of your progress, coins, streak and settings. Word Prism keeps no account, so there is nothing to close.

On our server. Two things can be there, each tied only to the anonymous installation identifier: your leaderboard record (nickname, scores, finished-puzzle count and game language), and, on Android, records of completed purchase orders matched to your installation (order number, product, purchase time, billing country — deleted automatically after 60 days).

How to have them deleted:

  • In the game: Settings → "Delete my leaderboard data" deletes your leaderboard record at once.
  • By email: write to ayanelabs@gmail.com with the subject Word Prism data deletion and tell us the nickname you used. We delete the records within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Nothing else about you is stored on our server — there is no account, email address or payment card to keep. Ordinary server access logs may keep an IP address for up to 30 days for security, after which they are discarded.
