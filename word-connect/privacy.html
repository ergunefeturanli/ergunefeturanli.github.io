Word Souvenir Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayane Labs handles information in the Word Souvenir mobile game.

Word Souvenir is an offline word puzzle game: you connect letters on a wheel to fill a crossword and collect postcards from cities around the world. The app does not require an account, a login, an email address, or a user profile. It shows no ads and contains no analytics. Your progress lives on your device. The only part of the app that talks to our own server is the occasional coin gift described below; the whole game works without it.

Information We Store on Your Device

Word Souvenir stores gameplay information locally on your device so the app can remember your level, unfinished puzzle, found words, postcard album, badges, score, daily streak, coins, helpers, and settings such as sound, music, vibration and language.

The app also generates a random identifier for your installation and stores it on the device. It is not your device's advertising identifier, and it is not linked to your name, email address, or any account.

This local game data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Coin Gifts

From time to time we give players free coins — to everyone (for example on a holiday) or to one player (for example to make up for a problem you wrote to us about). To learn whether a gift is waiting, the app asks an Ayane Labs server at api.ayanelabs.com when it opens. The request carries the anonymous installation identifier described above, which lets a gift be addressed to one installation; it is not your name, email address, phone number, or advertising identifier.

A gift for one installation is stored on the server with that identifier and deleted about a month after it ends. The app remembers which gifts it has collected; the server does not. The server is operated by Ayane Labs and hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Ordinary server access logs may keep an IP address for up to 30 days for security.

Update Check

The app occasionally downloads a small configuration file from GitHub Pages (ergunefeturanli.github.io) to learn whether a newer version of the game is available, so it can suggest updating. The request carries no identifier of yours; like any web request, it lets GitHub see your IP address.

Advertising and Analytics

Word Souvenir currently shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking services. If this changes, this policy will be updated before such a version is released.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email from the app, the message includes optional technical details: app version, platform, language, and your player code — the anonymous installation identifier — so we can help with a problem. You can delete any of these lines before sending. We use support emails only to respond to feedback, support requests, or bug reports.

Children

Word Souvenir is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

  • An Ayane Labs server (api.ayanelabs.com, Frankfurt) for coin gifts.
  • GitHub Pages for these legal pages and the update configuration file.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play services for app distribution, if you install the app through those stores. If you choose to rate the game, the rating window is shown by Apple or Google.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

On your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes every copy of your game data: progress, album, statistics, coins and settings. Word Souvenir keeps no account, so there is nothing to close.

On our side. We do not store your game data on a server. What we may hold: a coin gift addressed to your installation (deleted about a month after it ends) and any email you sent us. To have them deleted, email ayanelabs@gmail.com with the subject Word Souvenir data deletion; tell us your player code (it is in the email the app prepares from Settings → Write to us); we delete them within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at ayanelabs@gmail.com.
