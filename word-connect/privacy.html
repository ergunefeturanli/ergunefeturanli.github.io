Word Souvenir Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayane Labs handles information in the Word Souvenir mobile game.

Word Souvenir is an offline word puzzle game: you connect letters on a wheel to fill a crossword and collect postcards from cities around the world. The app does not require an account, a login, an email address, or a user profile. It shows ads (see Advertising) and offers optional in-app purchases; it contains no analytics. Your progress lives on your device. The app talks to our own server only for the leaderboard, the occasional coin gift and delivering some purchases, all described below; the whole game works without them. While your device is offline the game shows no ads.

Information We Store on Your Device

Word Souvenir stores gameplay information locally on your device so the app can remember your level, unfinished puzzle, found words, postcard album, badges, score, daily streak, coins, helpers, and settings such as sound, music, vibration and language.

The app also generates a random identifier for your installation and stores it on the device. It is not your device's advertising identifier, and it is not linked to your name, email address, or any account.

This local game data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Leaderboard

Word Souvenir has a weekly and all-time leaderboard. To take part, the app sends the following to an Ayane Labs server at api.ayanelabs.com: your puzzle scores and the number of puzzles you finished, your display name, and the anonymous installation identifier described above.

Your display name starts as a random "Player" name with four digits; you can change it to any nickname in the leaderboard screen. Names are filtered for offensive words, and nothing checks a name against a real identity. Your display name, score and rank are visible to other players.

The identifier is not your name, email address, phone number, or advertising identifier; it exists so your own score is recognised as yours on a later visit and can be restored if you reinstall. The leaderboard also includes computer-generated players, so the board is not empty while the game is new. If you would like your leaderboard record deleted, see Your Choices and Data Deletion below.

Coin Gifts

From time to time we give players free coins — to everyone (for example on a holiday) or to one player (for example to make up for a problem you wrote to us about). To learn whether a gift is waiting, the app asks an Ayane Labs server at api.ayanelabs.com when it opens. The request carries the anonymous installation identifier described above, which lets a gift be addressed to one installation; it is not your name, email address, phone number, or advertising identifier.

A gift for one installation is stored on the server with that identifier and deleted about a month after it ends. The app remembers which gifts it has collected; the server does not. The server is operated by Ayane Labs and hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Ordinary server access logs may keep an IP address for up to 30 days for security.

Reminders and Notifications

If you allow notifications, Word Souvenir schedules reminders on your device: at most one ordinary reminder a day, a reminder on a day your daily streak would otherwise end, and a few reminders if you stop playing (on the 10th, 14th, 21st and 30th day), after which there are none. Never between 10 pm and 8 am. All of this is scheduled on the device itself; nothing about your reminders is sent to our server.

Reminders are off until you turn them on. You can turn them off at any time in the app settings or in your device's system settings.

Update Check

The app occasionally downloads a small configuration file from GitHub Pages (ergunefeturanli.github.io) to learn whether a newer version of the game is available, so it can suggest updating. The request carries no identifier of yours; like any web request, it lets GitHub see your IP address.

Advertising

Word Souvenir uses Google AdMob to show ads: a banner at the bottom of the game screen from level 10 on, occasional full-screen ads between levels from level 11 on, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for coins. While your device is offline the game shows no ads. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the banner and the full-screen ads; rewarded ads stay available if you want them.

Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app activity related to ad delivery, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data. This processing is handled by Google according to Google's policies. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Word Souvenir shows a consent message when the game opens, before personalized advertising is used. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads where this choice is available. If you do not allow personalized ads, ads may still be shown, but they may be less relevant. In those regions you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, Word Souvenir asks for permission to use Apple's advertising identifier through the App Tracking Transparency prompt when the game opens. If you decline, Word Souvenir will not access the IDFA for tracking, and the game works the same. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's settings.

In-App Purchases

Word Souvenir offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs, a one-time starter pack, and "Remove Ads". Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details, billing address, or store account.

We use Adapty to check which purchases an installation is entitled to. Adapty receives the anonymous installation identifier, the purchase receipt issued by the store, and basic device and country information. It does not receive your name or payment details.

On Android, a payment that the store completes later (for example a pending or cash payment) is delivered through our server. Google notifies us of each completed order through Adapty, and our server stores the order number, the product, the purchase time and the billing country. When your app reports that it is waiting for a payment, the server matches the order to it and records the anonymous installation identifier with the order, so the purchase is delivered exactly once. These order records are deleted automatically after 60 days.

Analytics

Word Souvenir uses no analytics or tracking services of its own. If this changes, this policy will be updated before such a version is released.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email from the app, the message includes optional technical details: app version, platform, language, and your player code — the anonymous installation identifier — so we can help with a problem. You can delete any of these lines before sending. We use support emails only to respond to feedback, support requests, or bug reports.

Children

Word Souvenir is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

  • An Ayane Labs server (api.ayanelabs.com, Frankfurt) for the leaderboard, coin gifts and delivering pending Android purchases.
  • Google AdMob for ads, including Google's consent message where consent is required.
  • Adapty for checking in-app purchases.
  • GitHub Pages for these legal pages and the update configuration file.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play services for app distribution, if you install the app through those stores. If you choose to rate the game, the rating window is shown by Apple or Google.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

On your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes every copy of your game data: progress, album, statistics, coins and settings. Word Souvenir keeps no account, so there is nothing to close.

On our side. What we may hold: your leaderboard record (display name, scores, number of finished puzzles and the anonymous installation identifier), a coin gift addressed to your installation (deleted about a month after it ends), Android purchase order records (deleted after 60 days) and any email you sent us. Ad data is held by Google; you can manage it through your ad privacy choices and your device settings. To have them deleted, email ayanelabs@gmail.com with the subject Word Souvenir data deletion; tell us your player code (it is in the email the app prepares from Settings → Write to us); we delete them within 30 days and reply to confirm.
