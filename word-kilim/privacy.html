Word Kilim Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayane Labs handles information in the Word Kilim mobile game (a themed hidden-word puzzle, published on the App Store as "Hidden Word Search: Word Kilim").

Word Kilim is a local-first puzzle game. It does not require an account, a login, an email address, or a user profile. Your progress lives on your device, puzzles are created on your device, and the game works without an internet connection. The parts of the app that use the network are ads, ad consent, analytics, in-app purchases and, on Android, the delivery of completed purchases, all described below.

Information We Store on Your Device

Word Kilim stores gameplay information locally on your device so the game can remember your level progress, unfinished boards, coins, badges, daily puzzle streak, statistics, language choice, purchases you own, and settings such as sound, music, vibration and reminders. This data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Reminders and Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Word Kilim schedules them locally on your device. Nothing about your reminders is sent to a server and no push token is registered. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's settings or in your device's system settings.

Advertising

Word Kilim uses Google AdMob to show ads: rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for a hint, and, from level 16 on, occasional full-screen ads between levels. There are no banner ads. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the full-screen ads between levels; rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app activity related to ad delivery, diagnostics, and advertising interaction data. This processing is handled by Google according to Google's policies. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Word Kilim shows Google's consent message before personalized advertising is used. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads. If you do not allow them, ads may still be shown, but they may be less relevant. In those regions you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → "Privacy choices".

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iPhone and iPad, Word Kilim asks for permission to use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, Word Kilim will not access the IDFA for tracking, ads are still shown but not personalized using that identifier, and the game works the same.

In-App Purchases

Word Kilim offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs, a one-time starter pack, and "Remove Ads". Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details, billing address, or store account.

We use Adapty to check which purchases an installation is entitled to. Adapty receives an anonymous identifier for your installation, the purchase receipt issued by the store, and basic device and country information. It does not receive your name or payment details.

On Android, a payment that the store completes later (for example a pending or cash payment) is delivered through our server. Google notifies us of each completed order through Adapty, and our server stores the order number, the product, the purchase time and the billing country. When your app reports that it is waiting for a payment, the server matches the order to it and records an anonymous installation identifier with the order, so the coins are delivered exactly once. These order records are deleted automatically after 60 days. The server is operated by Ayane Labs and hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.

Analytics

Word Kilim uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used in aggregate, for example whether ads and purchases succeed and which parts of the game are played. Firebase may process a Firebase-generated app instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from IP address, and in-app event data. We use this information to improve the game; we do not use it to identify you personally. Test builds, emulators and simulators do not send analytics. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email from inside the game, your mail app opens with a message you write yourself. To help us answer, the draft includes the game version, your device name, your operating system version and the game language. You can delete any of it before sending. We use support emails only to answer you.

Children

Word Kilim is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, for advertising and ad consent.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, for aggregate usage analytics.
  • Adapty, for verifying in-app purchases and entitlements.
  • An Ayane Labs server (Frankfurt), for the delivery of completed in-app purchases on Android.
  • GitHub Pages, for this page.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play, for app distribution and payments.

Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the processing by the services listed above, the game does not send your gameplay data anywhere.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

On your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes your progress, coins, streak and settings. Word Kilim keeps no account, so there is nothing to close.

On our server. Only on Android, and only if you bought something: records of completed purchase orders (order number, product, purchase time, billing country), deleted automatically after 60 days. To have them deleted sooner, email ayanelabs@gmail.com with the subject "Word Kilim data deletion" and your order number from the Google Play receipt. We delete the records within 30 days and reply to confirm.
