Word Opal Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayane Labs handles information in the Word Opal mobile game.

Word Opal is an offline word puzzle game: every letter on the board belongs to one hidden word, and you split the board into words. The app does not require an account, a login, an email address, or a user profile, and it does not talk to any server of ours. It shows ads (see Advertising) and uses Google Analytics for Firebase for aggregate usage statistics (see Analytics). It has no in-app purchases. Your progress lives on your device. While your device is offline the game shows no ads.

Information We Store on Your Device

Word Opal stores gameplay information locally on your device so the app can remember your level, unfinished board, found words, coins, hints, rewards (gems, gift boxes, bonus flask), badges, statistics, daily streak, and settings such as sound, music, vibration, reminders and language. This data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Word Opal shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for a hint, to multiply a bonus-flask or gift-box reward) and full-screen ads between levels. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and basic device information to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. This processing is handled by Google according to Google's policies. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Word Opal shows Google's consent message when the game opens, before personalized advertising is used. If you do not allow personalized ads, ads may still be shown, but they may be less relevant. In those regions you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → "Privacy choices".

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, Word Opal asks for permission to use Apple's advertising identifier through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, Word Opal will not access the identifier for tracking, and the game works the same. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's settings.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are created and kept on the device; no notification service of ours is involved, and the reminder can be turned off in Settings.

Analytics

Word Opal uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used in aggregate, for example which levels are finished, whether hints and ads work as intended, and which settings are used. Firebase may process a Firebase-generated app instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from IP address, and in-app event data. We use this information to improve the game; we do not use it to identify you personally. Test builds, emulators and simulators do not send analytics. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

Word Opal offers no in-app purchases. If this changes, this policy will be updated before such a version is released.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email from the app, the message includes optional technical details: the app name and version, your platform and operating system version, and the game language, so we can help with a problem. You can delete these lines before sending. We use support emails only to respond to feedback, support requests, or bug reports.

Children

Word Opal is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob for ads, including Google's consent message where consent is required.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase for aggregate usage analytics.
  • GitHub Pages for this page.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution. If you choose to rate the game, the rating window is shown by Apple or Google.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes every copy of your game data. Word Opal keeps no account and stores nothing about you on our side, so there is nothing else to delete. Ad and analytics data is held by Google; you can manage it through your ad privacy choices and your device settings. For an email you sent us, write to ayanelabs@gmail.com with the subject "Word Opal data deletion"; we delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.

Contact
